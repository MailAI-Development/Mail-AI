Questions

Which email accounts work?

Any Microsoft account: Microsoft 365 work accounts and Outlook.com. It works with classic Outlook, the new Outlook and Outlook on the web, because Mail AI reads your mail through Microsoft's own sign-in, not through an app on your PC.

What can Mail AI see, and is anything stored?

Mail AI asks Microsoft for read-only access to your mail. It reads the folder and dates you pick, sends the text of position emails for extraction, and shows you the results. Email content isn't stored. The privacy page has the details.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Mail AI runs in your browser at mailai.uk/app, so you only need to sign in with Microsoft.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days and up to 100 position emails a day, with no card. When the trial ends, pick a plan to keep going.

Can I change plan or cancel?

Yes, at any time, from Manage in the app. Upgrades apply straight away, and if you cancel you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
