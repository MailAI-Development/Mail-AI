The service

Mail AI reads position and tonnage emails in a mailbox you connect and extracts open vessels into a table. It is a tool for professionals in the shipping industry. By using it you confirm you're using it for your business.

Extraction is automated and can be wrong or incomplete: a field can be missed or misread, and zones are assigned by rules. Always check positions against the original email before relying on them for a fixture or any other decision. Each row links to its source email for this reason.
