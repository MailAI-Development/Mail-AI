Privacy

What Mail AI reads, where it sends it and what it keeps.

The short version

  • Mail AI has read-only access to your mail. It can't send, move or delete anything.
  • It only reads the folder and dates you choose, when you click Extract positions.
  • The text of position emails is sent for extraction and the results come back to your browser. We don't store your emails or the extracted positions.
  • We keep a small account record: who you are, your plan, and how many emails you processed each day.

What Mail AI can access

You sign in with your Microsoft account. Microsoft asks you to allow two permissions: signing you in and reading your profile (name and email address), and reading your mail (Mail.Read, read-only). Mail AI does not ask for permission to send email, change your mailbox, or read your files or calendar.

You can withdraw access at any time from your Microsoft account: for work accounts at myapps.microsoft.com, and for personal accounts at account.live.com/consent/Manage.

What happens when you extract positions

  1. Your browser fetches the emails in the folder and date range you picked, directly from Microsoft.
  2. A quick check in your browser skips emails that don't look like position or tonnage lists. Those never leave your browser.
  3. For the rest, the subject and plain text body are sent to Mail AI's server, which removes boilerplate (bunker clauses, signatures and the like) and sends the text to OpenAI's API to extract the vessels.
  4. The vessels come back to your browser and are shown in the table. Mail AI's server does not save the email text or the results.
  5. Your browser saves the positions it found, with each email's sender, subject and date, so you can search them later and the same email is never extracted twice. They stay on your computer for the period you choose (14 days unless you change it), and you can clear them at any time.

OpenAI processes this text under its API data terms: API data isn't used to train its models and may be kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring before deletion.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe; Mail AI never sees your card number. We receive your subscription status, plan and renewal date.

Your choices
