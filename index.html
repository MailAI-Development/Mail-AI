The pipeline

From inbox
to spreadsheet.

01
Read emails
Connects directly to Outlook via COM API. Filters by email, folder and date range.
02
AI extraction
GPT processes each email and extracts vessel name, deadweight, build year, port, and open date.
03
Parse & clean
Regex normalises output. Inconsistent formats are standardised automatically.
04
Deduplicate
Fuzzy name matching removes duplicate vessel entries across sessions.
05
Write to Excel
Structured data is appended to your spreadsheet with port zone lookup.

Capabilities

Built for the
shipping desk.

01
Live listening
Monitor your inbox in real-time. New emails are processed the moment they arrive, with a pause/resume toggle.
02
Smart deduplication
MV prefix variants, ordinal dates, and fuzzy name matches are all normalised before duplicate checking.
03
Port zone mapping
Every extracted port is automatically mapped to its trade zone using the World Port Index.
04
Bilingual interface
Full English and simplified Chinese UI with live switching. Designed for international shipping desks.
05
Robust parsing
Regex-based field extraction means one malformed AI response never breaks the entire batch.
06
Excel native
Data writes directly to your existing .xlsx file. No new tools, no exports — it just appears in your spreadsheet.
